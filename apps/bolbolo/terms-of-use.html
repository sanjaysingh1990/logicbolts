The short version

  • A parent or guardian sets BolBolo up and stays in charge.
  • It is for your family's own learning, not for resale or classroom use.
  • Do not copy our content or try to get around the parent gate, limits or locks.
  • Rewards in the game are earned by learning and have no cash value.

1. What this page is

These Terms of Use are the everyday rules for using the BolBolo app and website. They sit alongside our Terms & Conditions, which cover accounts, purchases and legal responsibilities, and our Privacy Policy. If the two ever conflict, the Terms & Conditions apply.

2. A grown-up is in charge

  • A parent or guardian sets up BolBolo and adds each child. Children do not create accounts.
  • Please enter each child's real age group, so activities are pitched at the right level.
  • The Parent Zone is for adults. Do not share the answer to the parent gate with your child or help them through it.
  • You decide how long and when your child plays. We suggest staying nearby for the youngest learners, especially for speaking activities.

3. Using BolBolo well

  • Use BolBolo for your own family's learning, not for a business, a class or a group, unless we have agreed that with you in writing.
  • Keep the app updated so your child gets fixes and new content.
  • Use a child's first name or a nickname for their profile. A full name is never needed.
  • Let your child play their own profile, so their progress and reports stay meaningful.

4. Things you must not do

  • Copy, record, resell or redistribute BolBolo's activities, stories, songs, artwork or characters
  • Reverse engineer, decompile or modify the app, or try to get at its source code, except where the law expressly allows it
  • Work around the parent gate, screen-time limits, locks on content, or payment for Premium
  • Use bots, scripts or other tricks to earn stars, gems or badges, or to load our servers
  • Try to access another family's account or data, or probe or disrupt our systems
  • Use the Service for anything unlawful, harmful to children, or that infringes someone else's rights
  • Remove or hide copyright, trademark or other notices

5. Microphone and speaking activities

Speaking activities need the microphone, and your device will ask for your permission the first time. BolBolo listens only while the microphone button is active and does not keep recordings. If you say no, your child can still play everything that does not need speaking. Feedback on speaking is automatic and is meant as encouragement, not as an assessment.

6. Screen time and wellbeing

BolBolo includes a daily limit, an eye-rest break and a bedtime lock, and they are on by default. They are tools to help you, not a guarantee: please set limits that suit your child and check in on how they are using the app. If a limit screen appears, only an adult should add more time.

7. Rewards inside the game

Stars, gems, badges and shop items are part of the game. They are earned by learning, cannot be bought with money, and cannot be swapped, sold or cashed in. We may change how they work to keep the game fair and fun.

8. Offline play and devices

BolBolo keeps working without internet for content already opened on the device and sends progress up when the connection returns. If you delete the app or clear its data before it has synced, progress made offline may be lost. You are responsible for your device, your connection and any data charges.

9. Feedback and ideas

We love hearing from families. If you send us suggestions, you agree we may use them to improve BolBolo without owing you payment or credit. Please do not send us anything confidential, or any information about your child that we have not asked for.

10. If something goes wrong

If you find a mistake in a lesson, a bug, or anything that seems unsuitable for a child, please tell us at logicbolts@gmail.com. If you believe content in BolBolo infringes your copyright, write to the same address with enough detail for us to find it and confirm your rights.

11. Breaking these rules

If these rules are broken we may warn you, limit features, or suspend or close the account, as described in the Terms & Conditions.

12. Changes

We may update these Terms of Use as BolBolo grows. The effective date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you in the app about important changes.
