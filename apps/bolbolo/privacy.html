The short version

  • Accounts belong to parents. A child cannot sign up alone.
  • We store a child's first name or nickname, age, buddy and learning progress. No photos, location or contacts.
  • We do not store recordings of your child's voice.
  • No advertisements, no advertising trackers, and we never sell data.
  • You can delete your account and all your family's data from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

BolBolo is a spoken-English learning app for children aged about 2.5 to 9, operated by LogicBolts ("BolBolo", "we", "us"), Mohali, Punjab, India. We decide how and why the personal data described here is used. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") we are the Data Fiduciary.

This policy covers the BolBolo mobile app and this website. It is written for parents and guardians, because BolBolo accounts belong to adults: a child cannot create an account or use the app without a parent setting it up.

2. Parental consent

Only a parent or legal guardian may create a BolBolo account. By creating an account and adding a child profile, you confirm that you are that child's parent or guardian and you consent to us processing the child's data as described in this policy.

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the child profile or your account (see Your choices and rights). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

Areas of the app where settings, reports, purchases or outside links live are kept behind a "parent gate" that young children are not expected to pass.

4. Information we do not collect

  • Photos, videos or camera images of your child
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, calendars or files on the device
  • Advertising identifiers, and anything for advertising or profiling
  • Messages between users: children cannot chat with, or see, anyone else in BolBolo

5. Microphone and voice

Speaking activities use the microphone only while your child has tapped the microphone button, and only after you allow it on the device.

The sound is turned into text by the speech-recognition service built into your phone or tablet (provided by Apple on iPhone and iPad, and by Google on Android). Depending on your device and its settings, that service may process audio on the device or on the provider's servers under the provider's own privacy policy. BolBolo receives the resulting text and saves it with a score. BolBolo does not save, upload or share the audio itself.

You can switch microphone access off at any time in your device settings. Your child can still play every activity that does not need speaking.

6. AI-based speech feedback

BolBolo is designed to offer more detailed pronunciation feedback using an AI service from Google. This is optional and controlled by the "AI speech evaluation" switch in the Parent Zone settings.

When it is in use, only the learning item, the child's age group and the speech needed for scoring are sent. We do not send your child's name, photo or location, we do not keep the audio after scoring, and we do not allow the data to be used to build advertising profiles. If you switch it off, speaking is scored on the device only.

7. How we use information

  • To provide the app: accounts, child profiles, saved progress, rewards and reports
  • To keep learning working offline and sync it when you are back online
  • To enforce the screen-time limits you set
  • To provide Premium if you buy it
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and prevent misuse
  • To send you notifications you have switched on, such as a learning reminder or weekly report
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not sell personal data. We do not show advertisements. We do not track children across other apps or websites, and we do not use children's data for behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising.

9. Where data is stored and how it is protected

Account, profile and progress data is stored with our hosting provider in Mumbai, India. Some providers listed above may process data in other countries; where that happens we rely on the safeguards the law requires.

Data is encrypted while it travels between the app and our servers and while it is stored. Access rules in our database mean a signed-in parent can read and change only their own family's data. Access by our team is limited to people who need it to run the service.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your family's data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

11. Your choices and rights

As a parent or guardian you can, for yourself and your child:

  • See the data: the Parent Zone shows your child's profile and progress. You can ask us for a copy of anything else we hold.
  • Correct it: ask us to fix anything that is wrong.
  • Delete it: open Parent Zone → Settings → "Delete account & all data", or write to us and we will do it for you.
  • Withdraw consent and stop further collection by deleting the profile or account.
  • Switch things off: AI speech feedback, sound, notifications and the microphone can each be turned off.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you are unable to, where the law provides for it.
  • Complain to us using the contact details below, and to the Data Protection Board of India or your local regulator if you are not satisfied with our answer.

Write to logicbolts@gmail.com. We will ask for enough information to confirm you are the account holder, and we aim to reply within 30 days.

12. Children's privacy laws

BolBolo is built for children and is designed around the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the rules for children under the EU and UK GDPR, and the family policies of the Apple App Store and Google Play.

In practice that means: verifiable parental consent before a child's data is collected, collecting only what the app needs, no advertising and no tracking of children, no social features, and a simple way for a parent to see and delete their child's data.

13. This website

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above. If a change affects how we use children's data in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app or by email and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent again.
