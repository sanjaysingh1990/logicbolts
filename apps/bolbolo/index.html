Questions parents ask

What age is BolBolo for?

Children from about two and a half to nine. You choose an age group for each child, and the app adjusts button sizes, pace and difficulty.

My child doesn't speak any English yet. Is that okay?

Yes. The first world starts with greetings and single words, everything is spoken aloud, and menus can be shown in Hindi so the family can follow along.

Does the app record my child's voice?

No recordings are stored. When your child speaks, the phone's speech recognition turns it into text, the app compares it with the target sentence, and only the text and score are saved with their progress.

Does it work without internet?

Activities your child has already opened keep working, and progress made offline is sent up when the connection returns. Signing in and loading new worlds need internet.

How much screen time does it encourage?

Very little. The default daily limit is 30 minutes, with an eye-rest break every 15 minutes and a bedtime lock, all of which you can change.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

There are no ads. Premium is an optional purchase that only a parent can make from behind the parent gate. Gems inside the game are earned by learning and cannot be bought.

How do I delete our data?

Open the Parent Zone, go to Settings and choose “Delete account & all data”. Your account, every child profile and all progress are removed.
