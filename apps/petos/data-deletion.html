Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 3 October 2026

You can delete your Pet OS account and the personal data associated with it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Pet OS and go to Settings → Data Management.
  2. Choose Delete Account, review what will happen, type DELETE and confirm.

If you own a workspace that other people still use, transfer ownership first. If you have an active card subscription, cancel it first (App Store and Google Play subscriptions are cancelled in the store).

By email

If you cannot access the app, email logicbolts@gmail.com from your account email address with the subject “Delete my account”. We may ask you to verify your identity.

What happens

  • Your account is first scheduled for deletion and you are signed out on all devices. For 30 days you can sign in again and restore it.
  • After 30 days your profile, pet records, photos, reminders and workspace data are permanently deleted; backups are overwritten within a further 60 days.
  • Your subscription is not cancelled automatically if it was bought via Apple or Google. Cancel it in your store account first.
  • We may keep limited information where the law requires it, or to resolve disputes and prevent fraud (for example billing records).
  • If you own a family workspace, other members lose access to it. Export what you want first (Settings → Data Management).
  • Unused rewards and referral progress are forfeited when the account is deleted.
  • Deletion cannot be undone.
