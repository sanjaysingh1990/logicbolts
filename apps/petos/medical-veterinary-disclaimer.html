Medical & Veterinary Disclaimer

Last updated: 3 October 2026

In an emergency, do not use this app. Call a veterinarian now. If your pet is collapsed, struggling to breathe, bleeding heavily, seizing, bloated or retching without producing anything, unable to urinate, has eaten something toxic, or you think it may be dying, contact your nearest emergency veterinary clinic or a pet poison helpline immediately. Pet OS cannot call for help, and a delay caused by using an app can cost a pet its life.

1. Pet OS is not a veterinarian

Pet OS (the “App”), operated by LogicBolts (“we”, “us”), is an organisational and informational tool for tracking a pet’s health, medication, feeding, vaccination, weight, symptoms and appointments. We are not a veterinary practice. Nothing in the App, on our website, in our notifications, or in our communications is veterinary advice, a diagnosis, a prognosis or a treatment plan, and using the App does not create a veterinarian–client–patient relationship between you (or your pet) and us or anyone connected with us.

2. Always consult a licensed veterinarian

  • Talk to a licensed veterinarian before making any decision about your pet’s diagnosis, medication (starting, changing, skipping or stopping), diet, exercise, vaccination, surgery, euthanasia or end-of-life care.
  • Never disregard, delay or override professional veterinary advice because of anything you saw in the App.
  • Senior and chronically ill pets can deteriorate quickly and subtly. If you are worried, call your vet, even if the App shows nothing unusual.

3. Information, reminders and calculations may be wrong

  • Reminders and alerts. Medication, vaccination, feeding and appointment reminders depend on your device, its settings, the operating system, battery optimisation, network access, notification permissions and third-party push services. They can be late, missing, duplicated or wrong. Do not rely on them as your only safeguard. Keep a second method for time-critical medication.
  • Data you enter. Doses, schedules, weights, dates and notes are only as good as what you or another household member entered. Check every value against your vet’s instructions and the medicine label.
  • Calculated values and trends. Scores, averages, adherence percentages, weight trends, body-condition indicators, refill estimates and charts are approximations. They are not clinical measurements.
  • Reference content. Breed, age, vaccination and nutrition information is general. It may be incomplete, out of date, or not suitable for your pet’s breed, size, age, medical history or local law. Vaccination requirements vary by country and region.
  • Medication and drug information. The App does not check drug interactions, contraindications, allergies or correct dosage for your pet. Never give a human medicine to a pet unless your vet says so; many are toxic to animals.

4. AI-generated content

Some features (for example daily digests, summaries, insights and suggestions) use artificial intelligence provided by third parties, including Google Gemini. AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, out of date, biased, or simply invented (“hallucinated”), and it does not know your pet’s full history. AI output is not a diagnosis and must not be used to decide whether your pet needs treatment. See our AI Features Notice.

5. No guarantee of health outcomes

We do not promise that using the App will prevent, detect, diagnose, treat or cure any illness or injury, extend your pet’s life, or improve its condition. Every animal is different. Outcomes depend on veterinary care and many factors outside our control.

6. Reports and exports

PDF reports and exports generated by the App (for example health summaries, vaccination records and vet-visit preparation sheets) are compiled from information entered by users. They are not certified medical records. They may not be accepted as proof of vaccination by veterinarians, boarding facilities, airlines, border authorities or insurers. Verify with the relevant authority and rely on records issued by your veterinarian.

7. Third-party and family-shared data

If you share a pet workspace with family members, carers or others, entries made by them appear in your account. We do not verify who entered what, and we are not responsible for entries made by anyone other than us.
