AI Features Notice

Last updated: 2 October 2026

AI output is not veterinary advice. It can be wrong, incomplete or misleading. Never use it to decide whether, when or how to treat your pet. Contact a veterinarian, and go to an emergency clinic for urgent signs.

What the AI features do

Some features of Pet OS (for example the AI assistant chat, daily digests, summaries of logs and trends, and suggested questions for your vet) use generative AI. They read the pet records and text you provide and produce text.

  • Cloud AI uses Google Gemini (through our servers, or Google Firebase on paid plans). Usage limits depend on your plan.
  • On-device AI uses an AI model you download to your phone, or your phone’s built-in AI where available. It works offline and nothing leaves your device, but it is smaller and usually less accurate than cloud AI.
  • Each answer shows which AI produced it. If the AI you chose is unavailable, the app tells you; in Auto mode it may answer with another available AI and says so.

Limitations

  • Generative AI can produce statements that sound confident but are false, outdated or unsuitable for your pet.
  • It has no examination, laboratory results or full history, and can miss or mis-rank serious problems. An absence of warnings does not mean your pet is well.
  • It may reflect your data errors. Bad or missing entries lead to bad output.
  • Output can vary between runs, and may not be available offline or during provider outages.

How your data is used

Content is sent to the cloud AI provider only when you use a cloud AI feature; on-device AI sends nothing. It is used to generate your result, and we do not use it to train our own models. We ask providers not to train public models on it, subject to their terms and settings. See the Privacy Policy. Please avoid entering personal details about people.
