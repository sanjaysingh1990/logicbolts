FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Pet OS only for dogs?

No — it works for cats too. It's built around the needs of older pets, but any dog or cat can use it.

Does Pet OS replace my vet?

No. Pet OS helps you keep records and spot changes. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything, and its AI answers are general guidance. Always talk to your vet about your pet's health, and contact a vet straight away in an emergency.

Can my whole family use it?

Yes. Invite family members and caregivers to your workspace and give each a role. How many people can join depends on your plan.

How does the AI assistant handle my data?

You choose. Cloud AI sends your question and the relevant records to answer it; the on-device model runs entirely on your phone. You can turn off the use of health data for AI features in Settings. See the AI features notice.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese and Arabic.

How do I cancel or delete my account?

Cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account. Delete your account and data from Settings in the app, or see account & data deletion.
