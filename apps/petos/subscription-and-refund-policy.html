Subscription & Refund Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy applies to paid plans of Pet OS and forms part of the Terms of Service. Plan names, prices, features and any free trial are shown at checkout and in the app.

Billing and renewal

  • Paid plans renew automatically for the same period (for example monthly or yearly) until cancelled, at the then-current price, unless the checkout says otherwise.
  • In the iPhone and Android apps, subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their terms. They charge your store payment method, include any applicable tax (such as GST/VAT) and send your receipt. You manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store or Google Play account. Subscriptions bought on our website are billed by our payment provider (Stripe).
  • A subscription covers your whole family workspace.
  • If a renewal payment fails, the store may retry it during a short grace period, during which you keep access; if it still fails, paid features end and your account returns to the Free plan. Your data stays.

Free trials and promotions

A trial converts to a paid subscription at its end unless you cancel before it ends. Trial eligibility is limited to one per person (Apple and Google apply their own eligibility rules to trials and introductory offers). We may end or change promotions at any time.

Offer codes

Promotional codes for the apps are Apple or Google offer codes and are redeemed with the store (in the app, choose Redeem offer code). An iPhone code does not work on Android, and vice versa. An offer code gives a free or discounted period on a subscription; when it ends, the subscription renews at the normal price unless you cancel in your store settings. Offer codes have no cash value and follow the store’s terms.

Rewards and referrals

  • Rewards are time-limited access to a paid plan (for example Premium for 7 days) that we grant, for example through the referral programme or as a support gesture. They are not money, have no cash value, cannot be transferred or exchanged, and are not refundable.
  • Rewards of the same plan add up; a reward for a higher plan starts immediately. If you subscribe to the same or a higher plan while a reward is running, the remaining reward time is paused and resumes if your subscription ends. Saved or paused reward time must start within 12 months, or it lapses.
  • Referrals. Invite friends with your code or link. The programme’s current rewards, holding period, time limits and yearly cap are shown in the app (Offers & Referrals). A referral counts only for a new family that joins with your code within the stated time and makes its first paid charge; a refund within the holding period cancels the reward. You may not refer yourself, your own family, or use fake or duplicate accounts.
  • We may change or end the referral programme or reward amounts at any time; rewards already granted are not affected. We may withhold or revoke rewards obtained through abuse, fraud or breach of the Terms.

Cancellation

Cancel any time in the app or in your App Store / Google Play account. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period, and you keep access until then. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds

  • Except where required by law, payments are non-refundable, and we do not give partial refunds for unused time.
  • Purchases through Apple or Google are refunded under their policies; please request refunds from them.
  • For website (Stripe) purchases, contact logicbolts@gmail.com within 14 days of the charge if you were billed in error or after an unrequested renewal; we will review each request in good faith.
  • Consumer rights. If you are in the EU, UK or another place with a statutory cooling-off or consumer guarantee, those rights are not limited by this policy. You may be asked to acknowledge that digital content starts immediately and that this may affect a right of withdrawal.

Price changes

We will tell you before a price increase takes effect for your plan, and you may cancel before it applies.

Changes to plans

We may change or discontinue plan features. If we remove a material paid feature, we will give you a reasonable notice and remedy as the law requires.
