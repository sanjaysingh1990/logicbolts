Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how LogicBolts (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use Pet OS (the “Service”). We act as the controller of your personal information. Contact: logicbolts@gmail.com, [REGISTERED ADDRESS].

Summary We collect what you enter (account, pet and health-tracking records, photos), basic device and usage data, and push-notification tokens. We store data with our cloud provider, and send selected content to an AI provider only when you use cloud AI features (on-device AI keeps everything on your phone). Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. The Free plan shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising identifier only with your permission; paid plans have no ads. Your pet’s health records are never used for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and maintain the Service: create your account, store and sync your records, generate reports, and send reminders and notifications.
  • To provide AI features you choose to use (see section 4).
  • To process subscriptions, apply store offer codes, and prevent fraud.
  • To run the referral programme and issue rewards (time-limited plan access).
  • To show ads to Free-plan users and measure them (see section 3a). Paid plans see no ads.
  • To secure the Service, diagnose problems, fix bugs and improve reliability and features.
  • To provide customer support and respond to you.
  • To send service messages (for example security, billing and policy notices). Marketing messages, if any, are optional and you can opt out.
  • To comply with law and enforce our terms.

Where the GDPR / UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performance of our contract with you; our legitimate interests in securing and improving the Service; your consent (for example push notifications, personalised ads, optional data sharing, and camera/photo access); and legal obligation.

3. How we share information

We share personal information only as follows:

  • Service providers (processors) that act on our instructions: cloud database, authentication and file storage (Supabase); push-notification delivery (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service); subscription management (RevenueCat); payments (Apple App Store and Google Play in the apps, Stripe on our website); AI processing (Google Gemini); customer-support tooling.
  • Advertising (Free plan). Google AdMob receives the advertising data listed in section 1 to show and measure ads. Where Google uses data for its own purposes (for example ad personalisation), it acts as an independent controller under Google’s privacy policy.
  • People you choose. Members of a family workspace you create or join can see the pet records in that workspace according to their role. If you export or share a report, whoever you send it to can see it.
  • Referrals. If you join with someone’s invite code, that person sees your first name, when you joined and the referral status (for example “joined” or “completed”) — never your pet records.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law or valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety and the security of the Service.
  • Business transfers. In a merger, acquisition or asset sale, data may be transferred, subject to this policy.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

3a. Ads on the Free plan

The Free plan shows banner and occasional full-screen ads from Google AdMob at natural breaks (never during emergencies, triage or data entry). Before any ad loads we ask for consent where the law requires it (Google’s consent message in the EEA, UK and certain US states, and Apple’s tracking permission on iPhone). If you decline, ads are non-personalised. Paid plans and active rewards remove ads entirely. Pet health records and AI conversations are never sent to ad networks.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Showing personalised ads through Google may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising under some US state laws; you can opt out at any time as described in section 8.

4. AI features

You can choose on-device AI, which runs entirely on your phone: nothing you ask it, and none of your records, leaves the device. When you use a cloud AI feature, the relevant pet records and your request are sent to our AI provider to generate the result. We ask providers not to use this content to train their public models, subject to the provider’s terms. See the AI Features Notice. Don’t include personal details of people in free-text fields you send to AI features.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, and equivalent mechanisms, for transfers from the EEA, UK or Switzerland.

6. Retention

We keep personal information while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except that backups are overwritten within a further 60 days, and we keep limited records as needed for legal, tax, fraud-prevention and dispute purposes (for example billing records for the period required by law). Data in a shared workspace may remain available to other members until the workspace owner deletes it. See Account & Data Deletion. Data cached on your device is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and row-level security, and offer optional two-factor authentication and an app lock. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your device and credentials safe. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information we will notify you and regulators as required by law.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export (portability), restrict or object to processing of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. You can do much of this in the app (Settings) or by emailing logicbolts@gmail.com. We will verify your identity and respond within the legal time limit (generally 30–45 days). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

  • Notifications: change permission in your device settings or in Settings → Notifications.
  • Camera / photos: change in your device settings.
  • Marketing: use the unsubscribe link or contact us.
  • Ads: change your consent in the app’s privacy settings, turn off tracking (iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) or delete/reset your advertising ID (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads), or upgrade to a paid plan for no ads.
